When you switch from String to Kit

The switch happens in two stages, one audience at a time. Nothing is turned off in String until its Kit replacement is live and tested.

  • Until Dec 1Everything keeps running in String, the same as today

  • Thu, Dec 10Switch 2. New client leads go to Kit, from the forms on your website

On each switch day, Priscilla sends you your new Kit links. We update your website and the emails we build. You update anywhere else your links live, like your Instagram and LinkedIn bios.
